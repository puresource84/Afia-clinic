Dental Insurance & Claims

Insurance policies, pre-authorisation and claims management

Part of the Afia Clinic Suite — a comprehensive dental practice management solution built on Odoo 18, developed by ITCS (IT Corner Solutions).

Key Features

  • Insurance policy and insurer management
  • Claim creation linked to appointments and treatments
  • Claim status tracking (submitted, approved, rejected)
  • Co-payment and coverage calculation
  • Insurance reporting and reconciliation

Compatibility

  • Odoo 18.0 Community & Enterprise
  • Arabic (RTL) and English bilingual support
  • Designed for dental clinics in Iraq and the Arab world
